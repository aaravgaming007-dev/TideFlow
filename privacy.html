Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. TideFlow is developed under strict privacy-first principles. We believe your music preferences, playlists, and listening habits are your personal business.

1. Zero Data Collection

TideFlow is built without trackers, analytics SDKs, or data harvesting scripts. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your IP address, name, email address, or device unique identifiers.
  • We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer, or any commercial telemetry providers.
  • We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any user metadata.

2. Local Storage & Cache

All your app configurations, favorites, listening logs, and cached audio files reside exclusively inside the private SQLite sandbox on your local Android device.

You retain 100% control of this data at all times. Uninstalling the application or clearing app data immediately purges all local storage.

3. Direct Network Communication

When searching or streaming, TideFlow sends direct HTTPS requests to public endpoints (such as YouTube Music InnerTube and community lyric providers) solely to fetch audio streams and synchronized LRC lyrics. These requests occur directly between your device and the destination endpoints without intermediate logging proxies operated by TideFlow.

4. Open-Source Verification

Our complete application codebase is public and verifiable on GitHub. Anyone can inspect, build, audit, or fork the code to verify that our privacy promises are faithfully maintained.

5. Contact & Inquiries

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via GitHub Issues on our repository or message the developer directly on Instagram: @aarav_sharma_sui.
