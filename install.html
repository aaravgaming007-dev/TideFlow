Installation Walkthrough

TideFlow is distributed as a clean, standalone APK directly from GitHub Releases. Follow this quick 3-step setup to get up and running on any Android 8.0+ device.

1

Download Official APK Binary

Download the latest release package directly to your Android device from our Releases Page or the button below. Always verify that you are downloading from github.com/aaravgaming007-dev/TideFlow.

2

Allow "Install Unknown Apps"

When opening the APK, Android may prompt: "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source". Tap Settings and toggle on "Allow from this source" for your browser or file manager.

3

Complete Installation & Enjoy

Tap Install. Once completed, open TideFlow. You are immediately ready to search, stream, and download music—no Google login or account setup required!

Troubleshooting & FAQs

Because TideFlow is an open-source project published independently outside the Google Play Store, Google Play Protect may show a generic prompt. You can safely click "More Details" > "Install Anyway". You can inspect the source code anytime on our public GitHub repository.
Whenever a new release is published to GitHub, you can download the new APK from our Releases Page and install it over your existing app. Your songs, playlists, and cached downloads will be preserved seamlessly.
To ensure uninterrupted background playback when your screen is locked, go to your phone's Settings > Apps > TideFlow > Battery and set it to "Unrestricted" or "No restrictions".
