ARCHITECTURE & CAPABILITIES

Engineered For Pure Sound

A modern client designed to extract high-fidelity streams from the YouTube Music ecosystem without algorithmic manipulation, paywalls, or privacy trade-offs.

Direct Stream Extraction Core

TideFlow communicates directly with the YouTube Music InnerTube backend to resolve original Opus and AAC audio streams without proxy bottlenecks. Enjoy instant playback latency, adaptive bitrates up to 256 kbps, and zero advertisement injection at the network transport layer.

Dynamic Material You

Built from the ground up for Android 12+ Monet design guidelines. The entire UI color palette smoothly re-tints in real time based on dominant album art colors and your device system wallpaper.

Real-Time Synced Lyrics

Native LRC parser with synchronized word-by-word karaoke-style glowing highlights. Automatically queries multiple global lyric repositories when embedded metadata is missing.

Audiophile DSP & Gapless Audio

Integrated 10-band graphic equalizer with customizable presets, sub-bass booster, stereo surround spatializer, and sample-accurate gapless transitions for live albums and continuous mixes.

Offline Cache & Storage

Download entire albums and playlists directly to your local device memory. Listen seamlessly on flights and off-grid areas with zero DRM restrictions or expiration timers.

Zero Telemetry & Tracking

No Google login required. No Firebase analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no user fingerprinting. Your listening history and playlists stay strictly on your device.

Android Auto & Background Service

Full Android Auto integration for safe vehicle media control, complete with Android lock screen media session controls, quick notification action tiles, and headset button mapping.

Playlist Import & Sync

Easily import your existing playlists from Spotify, YouTube, and local M3U files. Export backups at any time in open JSON formats with zero vendor lock-in.

Engine Specifications

Framework

Native Kotlin / Jetpack Compose with reactive state management and hardware-accelerated Canvas rendering.

Audio Engine

ExoPlayer / Media3 core with low-latency audio track routing, ReplayGain volume normalization, and Opus decoder.

Local Database

Room Database SQLite sandbox for instant playlist caching, favorites management, and listening history indexing.

Open Source

GPL-3.0 licensed. Based on the community-driven Vivi Music core with custom Material Design 3 enhancements.
